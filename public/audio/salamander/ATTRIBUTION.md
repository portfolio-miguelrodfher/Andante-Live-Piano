# Salamander Grand Piano Sample Subset

Andante Live Piano includes a web-optimized MP3 subset of the Salamander Grand Piano V3 samples for its default Concert Grand Piano playback instrument.

- Original instrument: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm
- Original license: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
- Original project/source: https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander/
- Packaged subset source: `@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity3@1.0.5`
- Subset used here: one velocity layer, minor-third anchors from A0 through C8

The score's MusicXML/MIDI instrument assignment is intentionally not used for default playback. Andante defaults to this concert grand sample set.
